## Checkpoint model

A later successful workflow that changes only governance/review documentation may therefore become the current **governance-package checkpoint** without replacing the underlying product-behavior evidence recorded here.

## Material defects found and corrected through the current product-behavior checkpoint

The tested branch has corrected the following material defects and integrity gaps:

- Companion assets existed but were not loaded by `index.html`.
- Broad legacy text replacement altered the distinct phrase “Independently verified.”
- Recruiter-email safety controls initially rejected the risk phrases the feature needed to analyze.
- W-2 contract wording initially collapsed into a generic contract classification.
- Sprint progress bar lacked an accessible name.
- Companion outline actions had insufficient contrast.
- Agent-permission descriptions were slightly below the WCAG contrast threshold.
- Memory-first reassurance text inherited dark text on a dark background.
- Narrow-screen navigation hid later product areas behind horizontal scrolling.
- Help Tips created nested focus stops inside already-focusable navigation buttons.
- Restoring an open Career Companion could transfer focus without a current-session user action.
- Recruiter-email caution detection and reply behavior were inconsistent for shortened links and guaranteed/instant-hire wording.
- Experience intake under-prompted context, scope, constraints, and outcome evidence.
- Resume gate language could feel unnecessarily judgmental.
- The original resume-component workflow did not provide a transparent local evidence-only draft helper.
- A held opportunity could still influence active priority, readiness, or Next Best Action.
- Manual `Mark complete` could award progress without completing the underlying workflow state.
- Opportunity recommendations did not surface the user-recorded next step.
- Returning workspaces could retain readiness points from legacy recommendation acknowledgments created by the removed manual-completion control.

These corrections are represented by the passing run 90 regression suite. Human comprehension and judgment evidence remain separate gates.
